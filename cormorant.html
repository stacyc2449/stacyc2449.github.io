Cormorant

I finally started trying to design origami. This is my first design, made by modifying traditional bases. It's a mix of a bird base and a frog base, with a sink in the center. Crease pattern and folded base (without the sink) made on Oriedita
If I were to do it again, I would want a different way to sink the center, since it is a little awkward - but it's the most important part for the structure.

Full instructions probably never getting made :( but it is quite replicable by the images
